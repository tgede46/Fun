# Meditation

## What Success Looks Like

At work phase end: OS notification + overlay scrim on canvas + centered modal with short guided text. User closes via « Reprendre » or Esc (focus trap, EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor). Canvas non-editable during overlay `[ASSUMPTION]`.

## Your Approach

Guided text — short, 3–5 breaths worth:

> Pause — respire.  
> Inspire lentement…  
> Expire…  
> Reprends quand tu es prêt.

No gamification, no streaks, no guilt if skipped.

Modal: focus trap, Esc closes, contrast AA on themes.

## Non-Inferables

- MVP includes meditation in pause (UX memlog decision)
- Not a full meditation app — transition ritual only
